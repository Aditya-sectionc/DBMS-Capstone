Phase 5 — Real-World Use Cases, Scaling & Deliverable Checklist (20 Marks)
Enterprise scaling architecture plus interactive checklist & CSV exports.

          

            
            
          

        

        

          

            
Scaling to an Enterprise Organization

            
Range Partitioning & Archival: Partition Attendance and Payroll by (pay_year, pay_month) and archive historical years (>24 months) to cold storage.

            
Idempotent Batch Payroll Jobs: Process monthly payroll in department chunks using UNIQUE(emp_id, pay_month, pay_year) and pre-disbursement approval locks.

            
Concurrency & Row-Level Locking: Wrap salary revisions and leave approvals in READ COMMITTED transactions with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE.

            
Defense-in-Depth & Audit Trails: Pair application RBAC with DB roles, parameterized queries, and immutable audit tables.
